CS506 Final Project proposal

Alex C and Sai K

Goal: 
Measure how accurate Polymarket's prices really are as forecasts, and determine who profits when they're wrong. 

Description:
Polymarket is a popular online betting platform that allows users to bet on events of all types - from sports to weather to politics and social media. They are marketed through two avenues: one promises a way for people (especially young people) to make quick money. Polymarket has paid hundreds of content creators to advertise themselves making huge money off tiny bets from things like “will donald trump say mcdonalds in his next press conference”. Polymarket as well as its rival Kalshi also market themselves as a forecasting platform that is “the true opinion of people” and more transparent than biased news sources. During the election people would routinely compare the polls to the polymarket odds – stating that the polymarket was a less biased source of truth. Polymarket’ design incentivises people with non-public information as they claim it creates a more accurate line. At the core of this project we intend to figure out how accurate the polymarket truly is and for the millions of bets that are wrong - who is winning and how unfair the platform is. 


Data Methods 
Scrape the polymarket transactions which are all public as they are done on the polymarket blockchain and then compare with data from past events - political events, weather etc.
